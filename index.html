Research Observatory

Understanding Ecosystems in the Amazon Basin.

WAOSUE is dedicated to investigating storm-driven windthrow disturbances, quantifying widespread tree mortality, and understanding their long-term impacts on carbon exchange across tropical forests.

Data Access & Resources

WAOSUE is committed to open science. Our processed geospatial data, field inventories, and carbon flux measurements are available for global research collaboration.
