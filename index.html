Research Initiative

Understanding Storm-Driven Windthrow and Tree Mortality in the Amazon Basin.

WAOSUE is a collaborative scientific observatory dedicated to investigating large-scale windthrow disturbances, quantifying rapid canopy changes, and assessing their long-term impacts on carbon dynamics across tropical forest ecosystems.

Open Access Datasets

We believe in transparent research. All processed field metrics, mortality censuses, and gap fraction data are permanently hosted and version-controlled.

Windthrow Disturbance Core Dataset

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.11168104
View on Zenodo

This complete repository contains ground-truthed field metrics, high-resolution canopy gap mapping data, and time-series disturbance records collected across our primary Amazonian study plots. Data is provided in standard CSV and spatial formats.
